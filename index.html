01

About this policy

This privacy policy applies to the Society-Agents research project conducted by thisishiu and team.

Society-Agents is a research project that studies publicly available online discussions, social trends, topics, interactions, and information propagation on social media platforms.

The project may obtain information from services provided by Meta, including the Threads platform and related APIs, where access is permitted by Meta and the permissions granted to the application.

The project is intended for research and analytical purposes. It is not operated as an advertising platform, data brokerage service, or consumer profiling service.

This policy applies to information obtained through authorized APIs, publicly available content processed by the research system, information voluntarily provided to the researchers, and technical information generated while operating the research infrastructure.

02

Information we collect

Depending on the APIs, permissions, and research experiments being used, Society-Agents may process the following categories of information:

  • Public social media content: publicly available posts, text, captions, timestamps, permalinks, and publicly visible interactions.
  • Public account information: usernames, account identifiers, public profile names, and other profile information made available through an authorized API.
  • Content metadata: post identifiers, publication timestamps, media types, publicly available URLs, and similar technical metadata.
  • Research-derived information: topic classifications, sentiment or semantic representations, embeddings, clusters, relationships between public posts, statistical summaries, and other analytical outputs generated by the research system.
  • Authentication information: OAuth access tokens and related authorization information when required to communicate with an API.
  • Technical logs: request timestamps, API error information, service logs, and other information necessary to maintain and debug the research system.

Information obtained from Meta services is received through Meta APIs and only through permissions available to and authorized for the application.

Society-Agents does not request or intentionally collect user passwords. OAuth access tokens must not be submitted through the data deletion form or by email.

The privacy policy website itself does not intentionally use advertising cookies or behavioral advertising technologies. Basic server logs may be produced automatically by the hosting infrastructure for security, diagnostics, and reliability.

03

How we use information

Data processed by Society-Agents is used for research purposes, including studying social discussions and trends and developing methods for processing social media information.

Research activities may include:

  • identifying topics and emerging trends;
  • grouping related posts and discussions;
  • natural-language processing and semantic analysis;
  • sentiment or opinion analysis;
  • entity and relationship extraction;
  • social or information network analysis;
  • time-series and trend analysis;
  • evaluation of machine-learning and AI models;
  • development of aggregate research statistics;
  • studying how information and topics change or spread over time.

Where practical, research analysis is performed using identifiers, pseudonymized records, aggregated statistics, or machine-generated representations rather than relying on a person's real-world identity.

The researchers do not use the information collected through this project to make decisions concerning employment, credit, insurance, housing, healthcare, or other similarly sensitive decisions about individual users.

Research findings may be included in academic reports, presentations, demonstrations, software evaluations, or research publications.

Published research will generally use aggregated statistics, anonymized or pseudonymized information, and representative examples where appropriate. The researchers will avoid publishing access tokens, private credentials, or other information that is not intended to be publicly disclosed.

04

Sharing & storage

Research information may be accessible to members of the Society-Agents research and development team who require access for research, engineering, evaluation, or system maintenance.

Information may also be processed using infrastructure or service providers required to operate the research system, such as cloud hosting, databases, storage systems, API providers, and AI or machine-learning services.

These services are used only where necessary to operate the research project or perform the relevant analysis.

Society-Agents does not sell personal information collected through the project and does not use the information for targeted advertising.

The researchers do not intentionally provide datasets containing identifiable user information to third parties for independent advertising, marketing, or data-brokerage purposes.

Research data may be stored on development machines, research servers, databases, or cloud infrastructure used by the project. Access is limited to authorized members of the project where reasonably possible.

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect research information, including access controls, restricted credentials, authentication mechanisms, and secure storage of API credentials where applicable.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and therefore the project cannot guarantee absolute security.

Raw research information is retained only for as long as it is reasonably required for the relevant research, verification, reproducibility, system development, or legal and platform obligations.

Unless a longer period is reasonably necessary for an active research project, raw identifiable research records are generally reviewed for deletion or anonymization within 12 months of collection.

Aggregated, anonymized, or statistical research results that can no longer reasonably be associated with an individual account may be retained for longer periods for research reproducibility and scientific analysis.

OAuth access tokens are retained only while needed for authorized API access and may be removed when access is revoked, an account is disconnected, the authorization expires, or the token is no longer needed for the project.

05

Your choices & data deletion

You may contact the Society-Agents team to ask a privacy question, request information about data associated with you, or request deletion of information maintained by this research project.

To submit a deletion request, email hqhz12345z@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request”.

Please provide enough information for the researchers to locate the relevant records. This may include a public username, account identifier, post URL, post identifier, or other identifier associated with the information you want removed.

Do not send your password, OAuth access token, or other account credentials.

The researchers may request reasonable additional information to confirm that the request concerns the correct account or data. Verification information will only be requested where reasonably necessary to prevent unauthorized deletion requests.

Valid deletion requests will normally be processed within 30 days after sufficient information has been received to identify the relevant data.

Data may be removed earlier where technically practical. Additional time may be necessary when information exists in backups, research archives, or multiple research systems.

Certain information may be retained when reasonably required for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, research integrity, or to document that a deletion request was completed.

Information that has already been converted into anonymous or aggregate research results and can no longer reasonably be linked to an individual account may not be capable of being identified and removed on an individual basis.

Where Meta provides account-level controls for connected apps or permissions, users may also revoke the application's permissions through the relevant Meta account settings. Revoking permission prevents future API access that requires that authorization but does not necessarily remove previously collected research data. A deletion request may therefore still be submitted using the email address above.
